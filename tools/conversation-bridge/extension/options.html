Chrome cap quyen host theo origin: mau https://chatgpt.com/* va mau https://chatgpt.com/c/<id> cho ra cung mot pham vi, vi thanh phan duong dan trong quyen host bi bo qua. Nen tien ich xin dung https://chatgpt.com/*. Viec chi mot cuoc hoi thoai bi cham toi la do ma nguon giu: URL da arm o tren, doi chieu chinh xac URL cua tab, doi hoi dung mot tab khop, va mot lan doi chieu location.href nua ngay ben trong trang truoc thao tac DOM dau tien.

Duong ong toi native host

Neu ban nap tien ich truoc khi dang ky native host (dung thu tu trong tai lieu), lan mo dau tien se that bai. Sau khi dang ky host xong, bam Ket noi lai — khong can nap lai tien ich, khong can khoi dong lai Chrome. Cau noi cung tu mo lai co chan khi host chet giua chung; het ngan sach thu lai thi no dung han va doi dung cai nut nay.

Hoa giai mot khoa giao da chay

Mot lan tiem that bai van chay khoa cua no — do la cai gia cua huong an toan at-most-once. Co hai so khoa: mot ben native host (tren dia), mot ben tien ich (chrome.storage). Xoa mot minh so cua tien ich khong hoi phuc duoc gi: host van giu khoa, va lan poll sau van dung o ALREADY_DELIVERED.

Nen hoa giai o day di qua ca hai so, cho dung mot khoa ban chon, va chi khi host xac nhan da go thi khoa cuc bo moi bi xoa. Bam lan mot de chon, bam lan hai de xac nhan. Sau do HEAD do co the duoc danh thuc lai dung mot lan nua.
